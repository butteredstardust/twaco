# Code of Conduct

twaco follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the
work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues,
pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@butteredstardust](https://github.com/butteredstardust). Reports are handled confidentially,
and the maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, or block participants, as the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
